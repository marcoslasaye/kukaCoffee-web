Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Munduk & Kintamani coffee special?

The mineral-rich volcanic soil and high altitude give the beans slower maturation, resulting in denser beans with distinct sweetness and balanced body compared to lowland commercial robusta.

Do you offer dairy-free milk alternatives?

Yes! We have oat milk available for all coffee and matcha drinks, plus fresh whole young coconuts and fresh fruit smoothies.

Are the bakery croissants fresh daily?

Yes, our butter, chocolate, and almond croissants are prepared fresh every morning to pair perfectly with your morning brew.
