Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Munduk & Kintamani coffee special?

The mineral-rich volcanic soil and high altitude give the beans slower maturation, resulting in denser beans with distinct sweetness and bright, complex flavors compared to lowland commercial robusta.

Can I buy coffee beans to take home?

Yes! We offer 250g whole bean bags freshly roasted with degas valves and harvest roast dates clearly stamped.
